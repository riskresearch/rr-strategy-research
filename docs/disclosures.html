Forward-test record

The performance pages report a forward test run on Interactive Brokers paper (simulated) accounts. No client or proprietary capital is at risk in these accounts. Order fills, financing and costs are simulated by the broker and can differ from those of a funded account. The record is published without adjustment, is unaudited and currently covers a short period that does not establish the programs’ long-run behaviour. Operational events that affected results are disclosed on the relevant performance page.

Hypothetical backtests

Backtest figures are hypothetical. They are produced with the benefit of hindsight by applying rules to historical data, do not reflect actual trading, and cannot fully account for liquidity, execution, financing or the psychological impact of real losses. They are shown only relative to the S&P 500 over the same period and are net of modeled trading costs but before any fees. Hypothetical performance has many inherent limitations, and no representation is made that any account will or is likely to achieve similar results.

Past performance — actual, simulated or hypothetical — is not a reliable indicator of future results.

Key risks

  • Market risk. The programs are always net long equity and can lose substantial value; they are not hedges or drawdown-protection products.
  • Leverage. Futures are leveraged instruments. Total exposure can exceed account value, which magnifies both gains and losses.
  • Model risk. Systematic rules can underperform and may behave unexpectedly in conditions unlike those that informed their design.
  • Operational risk. Data, software, connectivity or broker errors can cause positions to differ from intended.
  • Currency risk. The programs run in U.S. dollars; results in other currencies also move with exchange rates.
  • Specific instruments. SCRA may hold bitcoin and precious-metal futures, which can be highly volatile.
  • Tax. Tax treatment depends on each investor’s circumstances and jurisdiction and may change.
