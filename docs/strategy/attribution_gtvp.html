Performance Attribution — S&P500 Core GTVP Allocation (SCGA)

S&P 500 Core GTVP (SCGA) is a tax-aware program that holds a permanent physical S&P 500 core — a UCITS ETF that is never sold — and manages market exposure through a capital-efficient ES/MES futures overlay. It aims to compound with the U.S. equity market while deferring capital-gains realization on the core and adjusting risk through the overlay rather than by trading the underlying.
