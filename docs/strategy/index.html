About

RR Capital is an independent quantitative research initiative focused on systematic strategies. Our focus is on building rules-based investment programs that combine rigorous signal research, disciplined risk management, and transparent execution across regulated U.S. futures exchanges.

All strategies are developed entirely in-house, grounded in academic finance and empirical market research. Live account performance is reported without adjustment. We believe that long-term investor trust is built through verifiable track records and honest communication of both returns and risks.

Research Framework

The conceptual foundation underlying each program is documented in an open research publication. It covers where returns come from, how to size and combine risk premia, how to estimate expected returns from current conditions rather than historical averages, and how to evaluate whether an active program earns its place alongside a passive core. The framework is structured in seven sections, from market prices and expectations through signals, portfolio construction, and live program deployment.

Active Programs

S&P500 Core Relative Allocation (SCRA)

U.S. large-cap equity futures (ES) form a permanent equity core, with dynamic allocation of up to half the portfolio to nine alternatives across equities, fixed income, real assets, and digital assets based on their risk-adjusted performance relative to the core. Position sizing uses downside-volatility targeting and dynamic leverage in the 10–20 % negative-volatility range, so each instrument contributes a comparable level of risk while the equity core always holds the residual.

S&P500 Core GTVP Allocation (SCGA)

A tax-efficient strategy built around a permanent equity core that is never sold, with a capital-efficient futures sleeve that dynamically increases or reduces risk. By holding the underlying indefinitely rather than selling to de-risk, the program defers capital-gains realisation while still managing exposure through the overlay. It can be deployed with a physical equity core (a broad global or U.S. equity ETF, USD or EUR), or as a futures-only implementation through a dedicated investment vehicle.

For an effective deployment, the suggested minimum investment per program is $500,000 USD. This allows positions to be sized in whole contracts with acceptable granularity and keeps execution and financing costs proportionate.
