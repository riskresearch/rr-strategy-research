01Approach

Built around the asset class that compounds.

Our work centres on volatility management because equity is the return we want to own — and the path it takes is what most often stops investors from keeping it.

i.

Equity is the engine

Over long horizons, owning businesses has been the most dependable source of real return available to investors. The programs are always net long equity; they do not try to replace that return, only to hold it more efficiently.

ii.

Volatility is the variable

Market risk is not constant: calm and turbulent periods cluster and persist. Scaling exposure to prevailing risk aims to keep the ride steadier and to earn more return per unit of risk than a static allocation.

iii.

Rules, not forecasts

Every decision is systematic and pre-specified. We publish the reasoning — the what and the why — in full; the exact calibration stays proprietary.

What they are not

Not hedges, and not drawdown protection. Fast declines can outrun any risk rule; the programs can experience equity-like — at times larger — losses, and suit long-horizon capital that can stay invested through them.

02Programs

One anchor, two ways to hold it.

Both programs use the S&P 500 as their anchor and share the same philosophy. They differ in how the equity exposure is held.

Futures core · relative rotation

S&P 500 Core Relative Allocation

SCRA

A permanent S&P 500 futures core, with a capped portion of risk rotated into nine liquid alternatives — other equity indices, Treasuries, gold, silver and bitcoin — only while they lead the core on a risk-adjusted basis. Positions are sized to comparable risk through downside-volatility targeting.

  • Instruments10 U.S. listed futures
  • Equity biasAlways net long
  • Base currencyU.S. dollar

Physical core · futures overlay

S&P 500 Core GTVP Allocation

SCGA

A physical equity core — a UCITS S&P 500 ETF that is never sold — plus an S&P 500 futures overlay that raises or lowers total exposure. Risk is managed through the overlay rather than by selling the core, which is designed to defer the realisation of capital gains.

  • InstrumentsUCITS ETF + ES / MES futures
  • Equity biasAlways net long
  • Tax outcomeDepends on jurisdiction
