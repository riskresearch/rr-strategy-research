Approach

Why our work centres on volatility management — and why equity is the asset we prefer to own.

Equity is the engine

Over long horizons, owning businesses has been the most dependable source of real return available to investors. Our programs are always net long equity; we do not try to replace that return, only to hold it more efficiently.

Volatility is the variable to manage

Market risk is not constant: calm and turbulent periods cluster and persist. Scaling exposure to prevailing risk aims to keep the ride steadier and to earn more return per unit of risk than a static allocation.

Rules, not forecasts

Every decision is systematic and pre-specified. We publish the reasoning (the “what” and the “why”) in full; the exact calibration stays proprietary.

What the programs are not. They are not hedges and not drawdown-protection products. Fast market declines can outrun any risk rule, and the programs can and will experience equity-like — at times larger — losses. They suit long-horizon capital that can stay invested through them.

In short, the programs share the passive core’s return source but borrow the implementation toolkit of systematic alternatives. They are best classified as a systematic, risk-managed equity allocation — a complement to an existing core, not a replacement for it.

Programs

Both programs use the S&P 500 as their anchor and are presented together: they share the same philosophy and differ in how the equity exposure is held.

S&P 500 Core Relative Allocation (SCRA)

A permanent S&P 500 futures core, with a capped portion of risk rotated into nine liquid alternatives — other equity indices, Treasuries, gold, silver and bitcoin — only while they lead the core on a risk-adjusted basis. Positions are sized to comparable risk through downside-volatility targeting.

Strategy document Performance

S&P 500 Core GTVP Allocation (SCGA)

A physical equity core — a UCITS S&P 500 ETF that is never sold — plus an S&P 500 futures overlay that raises or lowers total exposure. Risk is managed through the overlay rather than by selling the core, which is designed to defer the realisation of capital gains (tax outcomes depend on each investor’s jurisdiction).

Strategy document Performance

Indicative minimum for an efficient implementation: about US$500,000 per program, so positions can be held in whole contracts and costs stay proportionate. The programs are not currently offered to the public.
