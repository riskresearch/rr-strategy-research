Strategy

ES Core Relative Allocation is a systematic long-only futures strategy that trades six USD-denominated contracts on regulated U.S. exchanges: the E-mini S&P 500 (ES), E-mini Nasdaq-100 (NQ), E-mini Russell 2000 (RTY), Gold (GC), Silver (SI), and 10-Year Treasury (ZN). Together these instruments span U.S. large-cap equity, growth-oriented technology, domestic small-cap equity, real assets, and fixed income duration — each representing a distinct economic exposure.

The architecture is built around a permanent allocation to ES as the equity core. The signal evaluates the daily risk-adjusted performance of each alternative instrument relative to ES and allocates up to 50 % of the portfolio to the strongest-performing alternatives; the remaining weight defaults back to ES. This ensures the strategy maintains net long equity exposure at all times while dynamically rotating toward assets that are outperforming on a risk-adjusted basis.

Position sizes are scaled by each instrument’s recent downside volatility so that every contract contributes a roughly equal level of realized risk regardless of the current volatility regime. This volatility targeting requires dynamic leverage: the portfolio runs above 1× notional in low-volatility, high-conviction environments and contracts below 1× in high-volatility or weak-signal periods. Leverage is a structural consequence of volatility control, not a return-enhancement tool.

Backtested results (2003–2026):

Over a 23-year backtest using continuous futures data, the strategy produced a 16.8% annual compounded return and a Sharpe ratio of 0.807, compared to 8.6% CAGR and a 0.530 Sharpe for a simple S&P 500 buy-and-hold. The maximum peak-to-trough drawdown was −30.2%, versus −57.1% for the benchmark — substantially better risk-adjusted performance with nearly half the downside.

How to Invest

The strategy is currently accessible to external investors exclusively through the UJNU Darwin on the Darwinex Zero platform. Darwinex Zero is a regulated environment in which investors can follow the strategy in proportion to the manager’s own live trading account, with all positions independently verified by Darwinex.

Fee structure: Darwinex Zero applies a 20 % performance fee on net positive performance above the high-water mark. There is no management fee. Standard futures transaction costs (spread and commission) are embedded at the broker level and are included in all reported returns.

Risk normalization: Darwinex applies its own VaR filter (6.5 % monthly VaR target) to all Darwin products. This means the performance published on the UJNU Darwin page reflects the risk-normalized version of the strategy, which may differ from the underlying strategy returns when actual portfolio volatility diverges materially from the target. Investors following UJNU are exposed to the VaR-normalized track record, not the raw strategy.

Future vehicle: We are actively working to establish an independent regulated investment vehicle that will offer direct exposure to the strategy’s returns using our own position-level risk framework, free from any platform-level normalization. This structure will allow investors to evaluate performance against the actual strategy parameters rather than a third-party risk adjustment. We will publish details here when the vehicle is operational.
